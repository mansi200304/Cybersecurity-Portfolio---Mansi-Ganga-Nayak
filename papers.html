Privacy-Preserving Techniques for Large-Scale Data Analytics

Mansi Ganga Nayak, Dr. Research Advisor

International Journal of Advanced Research in Engineering, Science and Management (IJARESM), 2024

This research presents novel privacy-preserving techniques for conducting large-scale data analytics while maintaining data confidentiality. We propose differential privacy mechanisms that enable secure multi-party computation without exposing sensitive information. The proposed methods demonstrate significant improvements in privacy protection while maintaining computational efficiency for big data applications.

Blockchain-Based Secure Voting Systems: Architecture and Implementation Environments

Mansi Ganga Nayak, Co-Authors

Submitted to Springers International Journal of Information Security, 2024

This paper proposes a comprehensive blockchain-based architecture for secure electronic voting systems. The system incorporates cryptographic protocols, tamper-proof audit trails, and decentralized verification mechanisms to ensure vote integrity and voter anonymity. Our implementation demonstrates enhanced security against tampering attacks while maintaining scalability and usability for large-scale elections. The work includes the patent-pending "Safecount" EVM system design.
